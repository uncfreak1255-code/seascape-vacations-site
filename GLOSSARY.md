# Seascape Website

The Seascape website helps guests choose a home and proceed to direct booking, and helps prospective owners evaluate management and request a property-specific review. This glossary distinguishes those decisions from completed reservations and management relationships.

## Language

### Homes and guest planning

**Home**:
A physical vacation-rental property with its own location, amenities, sleeping arrangements and guest capacity.
_Avoid_: Listing when referring to the physical property.

**Collection**:
The homes currently listed for guest booking on Seascape's website. A home under management may not yet be part of the guest collection.
_Avoid_: Every signed property, every managed home.

**Listing**:
The advertisement for a home on a booking channel. A listing describes the home; it is not the home itself.
_Avoid_: Home when referring to the advertisement.

**Guest trip**:
The guest's intended travel dates and group size, which may be only partly specified while they explore homes.
_Avoid_: Booking, reservation.

**Guest capacity**:
The maximum number of guests a home accommodates. Capacity alone does not establish whether the sleeping arrangements suit a particular group or whether its dates are available.
_Avoid_: Bed count, availability.

**Sleeping arrangements**:
The bedrooms, beds and other approved sleeping spaces available within a home. They describe how a group can sleep, separately from the home's maximum guest capacity.
_Avoid_: Guest capacity.

**Stay dates**:
The arrival and departure dates of a proposed or reserved stay. The departure date is the day the guest leaves, not an additional occupied night.
_Avoid_: Booked dates when no reservation exists.

**Stay availability**:
Whether the requested occupied nights and arrival and departure dates satisfy the home's calendar restrictions at the time checked. An available stay is not a price quote, a hold or a reservation.
_Avoid_: Confirmed booking, guaranteed availability.

**Unknown availability**:
A stay whose availability cannot be established because the necessary calendar information is missing or could not be read.
_Avoid_: Booked, sold out.

**Shortlist**:
A guest's selection of homes to compare or share with their group while deciding where to stay.
_Avoid_: Reservation, hold.

### Booking decisions

**Stay quote**:
The price and reservation terms offered for a particular home, dates and guest count. A quote is an offer to consider, not a reservation or a hold.
_Avoid_: Confirmed booking, guaranteed price without an explicit guarantee.

**Stay total**:
The quoted cost for the selected stay and full guest count, including mandatory fees and taxes. Optional extras require confirmation and must not be assumed to be included.
_Avoid_: Nightly rate, owner payout.

**Booking handoff**:
The guest's transition from the website to a home's booking channel, carrying their selected trip details where available.
_Avoid_: Completed booking, confirmed reservation.

**Booking checkout**:
The process in which a guest reviews a home's price and terms and provides the details needed to request or complete a reservation.
_Avoid_: Departure when referring to the booking process; confirmation when merely entering it.

**Confirmed reservation**:
A reservation accepted and confirmed by the booking channel for a particular home and stay. A website click or entry into booking checkout is not confirmation.
_Avoid_: Booking click, checkout visit.

**Direct booking**:
A confirmed reservation made through Seascape's own booking channel rather than a third-party marketplace. The channel alone does not establish savings for the guest or owner.
_Avoid_: Direct-booking click when referring to a completed reservation.

### Owners and management

**Prospective owner**:
A property owner or authorized representative evaluating Seascape's management service. Interest or a review request does not establish a management relationship.
_Avoid_: Managed owner, client before a management relationship exists.

**Owner inquiry**:
A genuine question or request from a property owner or authorized representative about their property and Seascape's service.
_Avoid_: Form test, outreach attempt, management agreement.

**Owner review request**:
An owner inquiry asking Seascape to review a property, listing or stated operating concern. A listing link is one possible starting point; an address or operating concern can also provide context.
_Avoid_: Completed review, management quote, signed client.

**Revenue review**:
The one-page review of the listing or statement an owner supplies, identifying what deserves a second look and what Seascape would check next. It is the deliverable offered as the 48-hour revenue review, not a promised revenue result.
_Avoid_: Revenue guarantee, payout forecast, management quote.

**Management quote**:
A property-specific proposal for Seascape's management fee basis, included services and any separate charges.
_Avoid_: Revenue review, universal management rate, owner quote when the meaning could be a testimonial.

**Management agreement**:
The signed agreement that sets the terms of Seascape's management of a particular property, including service scope and fees.
_Avoid_: Inquiry, review request, unsigned quote.

**Managed home**:
A property under Seascape's management, whether or not it has been listed for guests or hosted its first stay.
_Avoid_: Listed home when referring to the whole management relationship.

**Owner statement**:
A report of what a managed home earned, what was spent and the owner's payout for a reporting period.
_Avoid_: Revenue forecast, performance guarantee.

### Evidence for decisions

**Qualified owner opportunity**:
An owner-direct opportunity with evidence of the owner or representative relationship, an invitation to relevant contact and a factual property or business fit signal.
_Avoid_: Public host name, scraped lead, proven owner demand from qualification alone.

**Approved owner proof**:
Evidence whose scope, freshness and permission support a particular public owner-facing claim.
_Avoid_: Historical portfolio figures without current approval, illustrative example, performance guarantee.

**Illustrative example**:
An explicitly hypothetical example explaining how a review or decision works, rather than reporting an actual owner's result.
_Avoid_: Case study, testimonial, approved owner proof.
