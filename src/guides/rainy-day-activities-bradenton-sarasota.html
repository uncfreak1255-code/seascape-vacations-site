01

Best Rainy Day Picks in Sarasota

1. Mote Science Education Aquarium at UTC

Mote Science Education Aquarium near UTC is a good choice for a half-day outing with children. Add lunch or shopping nearby if the rain continues.

2. The Ringling

The Ringling’s Museum of Art, Circus Museum and indoor galleries offer plenty to see for adults and older children. Explore the grounds when the rain lets up.

3. Sarasota Art Museum or a theater matinee

For an afternoon of art or a live performance, visit Sarasota Art Museum or check the schedules at Florida Studio Theatre, Sarasota Opera and Van Wezel. These are good options for couples, grandparents and anyone who would enjoy a quieter outing.

4. UTC, St. Armands, or downtown dining

If you would rather browse shops and stop for a meal, head to the Mall at UTC for enclosed shopping near Mote. Save St. Armands for lighter rain, when you can walk between shops and cafes during breaks in the weather.

02

Best Rainy Day Picks in Bradenton

5. The Bishop Museum of Science and Nature

The Bishop Museum of Science and Nature is an indoor option near the Manatee River in Bradenton. It suits families and science fans who would prefer to stay in Bradenton rather than drive to Sarasota.

6. Bowling, arcades, and covered markets

If the kids are ready for something more active, try bowling at Lucky Strike Bradenton or an indoor arcade. Covered markets are another option for browsing between showers.

03

What to Do on Anna Maria Island When It Rains

For a short shower on Anna Maria Island, stop for coffee, browse the shops on Pine Avenue or Bridge Street, or settle in for lunch under cover. The trolley can help you get around between showers. If rain is forecast for most of the day, consider a trip to a museum, aquarium or shopping center in Bradenton, Sarasota or UTC.

04

Quick Decision Guide

  • Best with young kids: Mote SEA, the Bishop Museum, bowling, or UTC.
  • Best for adults: The Ringling, Sarasota Art Museum, a theater matinee, or St. Armands dining.
  • If you are staying near AMI: stay nearby for a short shower; consider an indoor outing on the mainland for a full day of rain.
  • When choosing where to stay: Bradenton and Sarasota offer a wider choice of indoor activities than the islands.
05

Rainy Day FAQs

What are the best rainy day activities in Sarasota?

Mote SEA near UTC, The Ringling and Sarasota Art Museum are good places to start. For a performance, check Florida Studio Theatre’s schedule. The Mall at UTC offers indoor shopping and dining; St. Armands Circle is better suited to lighter rain and breaks between showers.

What should families do in Bradenton when it rains?

Visit The Bishop Museum near downtown Bradenton, or try bowling or an arcade if the kids want something more active. For a longer outing, consider Mote SEA and the Mall at UTC.

Should I stay on Anna Maria Island during a rainy week?

For an occasional shower, there are shops, cafes and places to eat on Anna Maria Island. If the forecast suggests several days of rain, Bradenton or Sarasota may be more convenient for museums, aquarium visits, theaters and indoor shopping.

Stay near beaches and indoor attractions

From Bradenton or Sarasota, you can plan beach days on Anna Maria Island or Siesta Key and have museums, aquarium exhibits, restaurants and indoor shopping to choose from when it rains.
