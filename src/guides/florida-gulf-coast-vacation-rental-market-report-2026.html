Bradenton-Sarasota Vacation Rental Benchmark 2026

Reviewed August 19, 2026 · Source window: June 2022 through March 2026

Scope first: This historical portfolio benchmark covers 545 confirmed bookings selected from 1,492 reservation records across five Bradenton and Sarasota homes. The fixed source window is June 2022 through March 2026. It is not a Florida Gulf Coast market average or a forecast for another home.

The value of this small dataset is practical: it shows the questions an owner should ask about booking lead time, stay length, seasonal pricing, and source mix. It cannot establish a current regional trend or promise a result for a different property.

Questions this benchmark can help an owner ask

  • How far before check-in do bookings arrive for this home, by season?
  • What is the median stay length, and how does it affect cleaning and minimum-stay rules?
  • Which figures are gross rent, net rent, or owner payout after channel and operating costs?
  • Which property differences explain a rate gap: bedroom count, location, water access, pool, condition, or operating dates?
  • How much demand comes from each booking source, and what does each source cost the guest and owner?

Questions it cannot answer

  • A current occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, or supply trend for the full Florida Gulf Coast.
  • An Anna Maria Island, Siesta Key, Longboat Key, or Bradenton market average.
  • The expected revenue of a home that differs in size, condition, location, amenities, or operating period.
  • The effect of one amenity without a matched comparison and a consistent calculation method.
  • A guaranteed owner-margin gain from changing booking sources.

Read the method before using the numbers
