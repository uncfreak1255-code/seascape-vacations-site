01

Anna Maria Island & Cortez

Cortez

Tide Tables Restaurant and Marina

Tide Tables sits on the working waterfront in Cortez, across the bridge from Bradenton Beach. Fish tacos, mahi and gumbo are on the menu, with outdoor tables overlooking the Intracoastal Waterway.

Arriving by boat: The Bradenton tourism bureau lists Tide Tables as boat accessible. Call 941-567-6206 before heading over to confirm where you can tie up. Restaurant details

Cortez

Swordfish Grill & Tiki Bar

Swordfish Grill serves seafood on Sarasota Bay in Cortez fishing village. Outdoor dining and live music make it an option for a meal with time to linger by the water.

Arriving by boat: The restaurant welcomes boats at its dock. Call 941-798-2035 to check space and arrival instructions. Restaurant details

02

Bradenton & Palmetto (Manatee River)

Bradenton

Pier 22

Pier 22 overlooks the Manatee River beside downtown Bradenton. The menu includes seafood, sushi and steaks, with an outdoor terrace facing the water.

Arriving by boat: Arrange a short-term slip with Twin Dolphin Marina, within walking distance of the restaurant. Call the marina at 941-747-8300 for availability and fees. Restaurant details

Palmetto

Riverhouse Waterfront Restaurant

Riverhouse sits at the end of the Safe Harbor Regatta Pointe Marina pier on the Palmetto side of the river. Its ground-floor Reef & Grill serves lunch and dinner, with indoor seating and a waterfront patio.

Arriving by boat: Safe Harbor Regatta Pointe offers transient slips. Call the marina at 941-729-6021 to arrange dockage and confirm fees. Restaurant details

03

Sarasota & Longboat Key

Longboat Key

Shore

Shore's Longboat Key location is at 800 Broadway Street, south of Longboat Pass and west of Jewfish Key. Its bayfront setting pairs a seafood menu with views across the water.

Arriving by boat: Call 941-259-4600 to confirm dock space and approach instructions before leaving. Restaurant and location details

Longboat Key

Mar Vista Dockside Restaurant & Pub

Mar Vista has outdoor tables beneath buttonwood trees and a covered patio at the north end of Longboat Key. The menu ranges from burgers to seafood, with the bay just beyond the dining area.

Arriving by boat: Mar Vista lists a private dock with 14 slips and shore space for smaller craft. Call 941-383-2391 or hail Channel 16 to confirm space and conditions. Docking details

Longboat Key

Dry Dock Waterfront Grill

Dry Dock overlooks Sarasota Bay at the southern end of Longboat Key. It serves seafood with outdoor dining on the waterfront, farther south than Shore and Mar Vista.

Arriving by boat: The restaurant identifies its boat approach as Marker 6. Call 941-383-0102 to confirm docking arrangements before your trip. Restaurant details

04

Tips for Dining by Boat

  • Check the Tides: The waters around Anna Maria Island are heavily influenced by tides. Some restaurants have shallow approaches that become impassable on a negative low tide.
  • Designated Skipper: The Coast Guard and local marine patrol strictly enforce BUI (Boating Under the Influence) laws. Penalties are severe.
  • Mind Your Wake: Almost all restaurants with docks are located in active "Idle Speed" or "No Wake" zones.
