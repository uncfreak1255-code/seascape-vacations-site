Why SRQ is the Best Airport for Anna Maria Island

While Tampa International Airport (TPA) offers more direct flights, SRQ is much closer and less stressful. The airport is small, easy to navigate, and getting from your gate to baggage claim to your ground transportation is usually quick. During peak winter season, the drive from TPA can stretch with I-75 and bridge traffic. If SRQ has reasonable flight options for your dates, it is often worth checking before defaulting to Tampa.

Transportation Options

1. Rental Cars (Most Popular)

If you plan to leave Anna Maria Island during your stay - perhaps to visit Siesta Key, downtown Sarasota, Robinson Preserve, or mainland grocery stops - renting a car is recommended. SRQ has rental car counters in the baggage claim area, and the rental car lot is within walking distance of the terminal. If you are still choosing where to stay, compare Anna Maria Island vacation rentals, Bradenton homes near the beaches, and vacation rentals near Anna Maria Island before deciding how much car access matters.

2. Uber and Lyft

Uber and Lyft operate at SRQ. A ride to Anna Maria Island commonly lands in the $40 to $60 range, but prices can move with surge pricing, time of day, and your exact island stop. Follow the app and airport signs to the designated pickup area. This can be a clean option if you plan to rely on the free island trolley, bikes, or short local rides once you arrive.

3. Private Car Services and Shuttles

For groups traveling with a lot of luggage or those arriving late at night, booking a private car service in advance provides peace of mind. Local companies like AMI Taxi and Paradise Car Service monitor your flight and meet you at baggage claim. A private SUV holding up to 6 people generally runs around $80 to $100 one-way.

Driving Directions from SRQ to Anna Maria Island

There are two primary routes to get to the island from SRQ. Your GPS will likely direct you based on current traffic conditions, but here is what to expect:

Route 1: Via Cortez Road (Best for Bradenton Beach)

  • Exit the airport and turn right (north) onto US-41 / Tamiami Trail.
  • Continue north to Cortez Road W / 44th Ave W.
  • Turn left onto Cortez Road W and take it all the way to the water.
  • Cross the Cortez Bridge onto Anna Maria Island. You will be in Bradenton Beach.

Route 2: Via Manatee Avenue (Best for Holmes Beach and Anna Maria City)

  • Exit the airport and turn right (north) onto US-41 / Tamiami Trail.
  • Continue north through Bradenton to Manatee Avenue W / SR 64.
  • Turn left onto Manatee Avenue W and take it all the way to the water.
  • Cross the Anna Maria Island Bridge. You will arrive directly in Holmes Beach, the commercial heart of the island.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I fly into TPA or SRQ for Anna Maria Island?

SRQ is usually preferred if convenience matters. It is often 30-45 minutes from AMI, while TPA is much farther and more exposed to interstate traffic. TPA can still be the better choice when it has cheaper flights, better nonstop options, or arrival times that work better for your group.

Is there a public bus from SRQ to Anna Maria Island?

Technically yes, via public transit and transfers, but it can take much longer than driving and is not a great fit for most visitors carrying luggage. Once you are on the island, the free AMI trolley is useful for beach-hopping, dinner, and avoiding parking hassles.
