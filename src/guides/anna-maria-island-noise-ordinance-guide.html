01

What Are the Quiet Hours?

Quiet hours depend on the city where you are staying. The City of Anna Maria publishes quiet time from 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM, including pool use. Check the current local rules for Holmes Beach or Bradenton Beach if your rental is in either city.

As a courtesy to neighbors, turn off outdoor speakers and move conversations indoors by 10:00 PM. Your rental’s house rules may be stricter than the city’s requirements.

02

What Constitutes a Violation?

There is no single noise test for every city on Anna Maria Island. The City of Anna Maria considers factors including volume, time, duration, background noise and proximity to homes. An audible sound alone does not explain all of those factors.

  • Keep music and conversations low enough to avoid disturbing neighboring homes.
  • Follow the local quiet hours for pool use, patios and balconies.
  • Keep daytime gatherings considerate too; nighttime quiet hours are not permission for excessive daytime noise.

03

Fines and Penalties

A noise complaint may lead to an assessment by local police or code enforcement and a citation under the applicable city rules. Fine amounts and enforcement procedures depend on the municipality and circumstances; there is no single island-wide first-offense amount listed here.

Check your booking agreement for the property’s noise rules and the stated consequences of a breach. Consequences under a rental agreement depend on that agreement’s terms. Contact your host if a house rule is unclear before your stay.

04

How to Be a Good Neighbor on AMI

Most vacationers have no intention of causing a disturbance. Noise carrying over fences happens accidentally when guests do not realize how far sound travels over water or in quiet neighborhoods.

  • Move the party inside at 10:00 PM: When 10 PM hits, take the drinks and conversation indoors and close the sliding glass doors.
  • Turn off outdoor speakers: If you are enjoying the patio in the evening, turn off any Bluetooth speakers before 10 PM.
  • Mind the pool: Pools act like acoustic amplifiers. Splashing and talking in a pool carry directly into the windows of the house next door.

05

Frequently Asked Questions

What if a neighboring property is keeping me awake?

If you are disturbed by excessive noise after 10:00 PM, you have the right to contact the local police department via their non-emergency number. Let them handle the situation professionally rather than confronting the neighbors yourself.

Are the rules different for holidays like New Year's Eve?

Do not assume a holiday suspends local noise rules. Check the current rules for your rental’s municipality and any event-specific notices. Follow the house rules in your booking agreement throughout your stay.
