Flights to Anna Maria Island: Best Airports, Airlines & Tips

Updated March 2026 · By Seascape Vacations · 9 min read

Flights to Anna Maria Island land at one of four airports on Florida's Gulf Coast, since Anna Maria Island itself has no commercial airport. The closest option is Sarasota-Bradenton International Airport (SRQ), located 18–20 miles from Anna Maria Island with a drive time of 30–45 minutes. Tampa International Airport (TPA) sits 54–60 miles north and offers 400+ daily flights from 80+ nonstop destinations, making TPA the largest hub in the region. St. Pete-Clearwater Airport (PIE) and Southwest Florida International (RSW) in Fort Myers round out the choices at 35–45 miles and 109 miles respectively. Seascape Vacations manages six Gulf Coast rental homes near Anna Maria Island in Bradenton and Sarasota, and our guests ask about airports more than almost anything else. Here is what we tell them—no fluff, just the airport-by-airport breakdown with real costs, drive times, and the tips that actually save money.

Which Airport Should You Fly Into?

This is the first question every guest asks, and the answer depends on three things: where you're flying from, how many people are on the trip, and how much you care about drive time versus fare price. A couple from Charlotte with a nonstop to SRQ? Easy call. A family of five from Minneapolis with no SRQ route? Tampa wins on price every time.

SRQ: The Easy Choice

SRQ is our pick for anyone with a direct flight available. You land, grab your bags (usually within 15 minutes—the terminal is that small), pick up a rental car, and you're crossing the Manatee Avenue bridge onto Anna Maria Island before your checked bag would even show up at Tampa.

Eleven airlines now serve SRQ: United, Delta, American, JetBlue, Southwest, Allegiant, Breeze, Frontier, Avelo, Sun Country, and Air Canada. That's a massive expansion from just five years ago when SRQ had maybe four carriers. The 68 nonstop destinations cover most major East Coast and Midwest cities, plus seasonal routes from Toronto and Montreal.

SRQ fares often run higher than Tampa, especially from February through April. For a solo traveler or couple, the shorter drive can still be worth it. For a family buying several tickets, compare the complete fare, bags and ground-transport total against TPA. We break down the full SRQ experience—terminal layout, rental car tips, exact driving directions—in our SRQ airport to Anna Maria Island guide.

TPA: The Budget Choice

Tampa International Airport is one of the best airports in the country. Consistently rated top-5 nationally. Clean, well-organized, easy to navigate despite being 10 times the size of SRQ. And because 20+ airlines compete for Tampa passengers, fares are consistently lower.

TPA often prices lower because more airlines compete there. Compare the same dates and passengers at both airports, then add bags, ground transportation and the extra drive time. The fare gap is not fixed, and it does not always cover the longer transfer.

The trade-off is the drive. TPA to Anna Maria Island is 54–60 miles, and the time depends heavily on when you land. Midday on a Tuesday? You might make it in 65 minutes. Friday afternoon during snowbird season? Budget 90 minutes, maybe more if there's construction on I-275 South or the Sunshine Skyway approach. The drive itself isn't bad—it's mostly highway—but after a long flight with kids, that extra hour in the car feels longer than the numbers suggest.

When TPA makes sense: Families of 3+, budget-conscious travelers, anyone whose city has a nonstop to Tampa but not SRQ, red-eye arrivals (no traffic at 6am).

PIE: The Allegiant Option

St. Pete-Clearwater International Airport exists primarily because of Allegiant Air. Sun Country also has a few routes, but Allegiant dominates PIE with nonstop service from 60+ small and mid-size cities that the bigger airports skip entirely. Places like Rockford, Illinois. Belleville, outside St. Louis. Sanford, north of Orlando. If Allegiant flies from your hometown to PIE, compare the complete total—fare plus bags plus any seat charges—against a SRQ or TPA itinerary.

PIE sits 35–45 miles from Anna Maria Island. Drive time is 42–70 minutes depending on traffic through St. Petersburg. The airport itself is tiny and low-stress. No long security lines. No confusing terminals.

The catch: Allegiant flights are often limited to 2–3 departures per week on any given route, and schedule changes are common. Bag fees add up. Factor in the total cost—fare plus bags plus any seat upgrades—and compare it honestly against a Southwest fare into TPA, where two checked bags per person are included.

RSW: The Long Shot

Southwest Florida International in Fort Myers is 109 miles and roughly 2 hours from Anna Maria Island. That's a long drive after a flight. We don't recommend RSW unless one of these specific situations applies:

  • Your city has a nonstop to RSW but nothing into SRQ, TPA, or PIE
  • The complete fare and ground-transport total fits your budget
  • You're combining an Anna Maria Island trip with time in Naples, Sanibel, or Fort Myers Beach

If none of those apply, skip RSW. The 2-hour drive eats into your vacation time and adds gas and toll costs that offset most fare savings.

Money-Saving Flight Tips

We've watched thousands of guests book trips to this area. What the savvy ones do differently.

1. Compare departure days. Check fares for the days you can travel, including baggage and seat charges. No departure day guarantees a fixed saving.

2. Book 1–3 months ahead. The sweet spot for domestic flights to SRQ and TPA is 6–12 weeks before departure. Book too early and you'll miss sales. Wait until the last week and you'll pay a premium, especially in peak season.

3. Compare SRQ and TPA. Add baggage and seat charges to the fare, then include the cost and time of reaching your lodging.

4. Check Southwest separately. Southwest Airlines does not appear on Google Flights, Kayak, or most aggregators. Search the same dates on southwest.com. Southwest flies to both SRQ and TPA, and two checked bags per person are included, so add bag fees from other airlines when you compare complete trip totals.

5. Consider a PIE open-jaw. Flying into TPA and out of PIE, or the reverse, can unlock combinations a round-trip search misses. Compare the complete fare, bag fees, and ground-transport total for each combination before you book.

6. Set fare alerts. Google Flights lets you track any route with email notifications when prices drop. Set alerts for your preferred dates to both SRQ and TPA. Prices fluctuate daily, and a good alert catches the dip you'd otherwise miss.

Track the fares for your own itinerary. A fare found by another traveler does not establish the cost of your trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the closest airport to Anna Maria Island?

Sarasota-Bradenton International Airport (SRQ) is the closest airport to Anna Maria Island at 18–20 miles, about a 30–45 minute drive. SRQ serves 11 airlines flying nonstop from 68 cities. The small terminal means shorter security lines and faster baggage claim compared to Tampa International.

Should I fly into Tampa or Sarasota for Anna Maria Island?

Fly into SRQ if your city has a nonstop and you want the shortest drive—30–45 minutes to the island instead of sitting on I-275. Fly into TPA if you need more airline options or your city has no SRQ route. Add baggage and ground transportation to both quotes; no airport guarantees the cheapest complete total.

How much does an Uber cost from SRQ airport to Anna Maria Island?

Check the rideshare app for a current quote at your arrival time. Compare that total with written taxi or shuttle quotes, including luggage and passenger capacity. Most visitors rent a car instead, since Anna Maria Island vacation rentals are easier to enjoy with a vehicle for grocery runs and day trips.

When are flights to Anna Maria Island cheapest?

September and October usually see the lowest demand, and January and February can also be quieter before Presidents' Day. Spring break (March–April) and the Thanksgiving and Christmas weeks are the most expensive periods. Compare live fares for several departure days if your schedule is flexible; no month or weekday guarantees a fixed saving on every route.

Do I need a rental car on Anna Maria Island?

Most visitors rent a car. Anna Maria Island itself is small enough to bike, and the free island trolley runs the length of AMI. A car still helps for the airport transfer, grocery runs, restaurant trips, and day excursions from a mainland home. Compare a current rental quote, fuel and parking with the transport you would otherwise need.

Does Anna Maria Island have its own airport?

No. Anna Maria Island does not have a commercial airport. The island is a 7-mile barrier island connected to the mainland by two bridges (Manatee Avenue and Cortez Road). The closest airport is SRQ at 18–20 miles. There are no ferry services from airports to Anna Maria Island—ground transportation (car, Uber, or shuttle) is the only way to get from the airport to the island.
