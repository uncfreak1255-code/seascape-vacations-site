Anna Maria Island vs Longboat Key

Two barrier islands separated by one bridge and a completely different vibe. What matters is how each one handles your beach time, dining, and daily logistics.

{{ uiIcon("info") }} Quick Answer

Anna Maria Island is a good fit for families and groups who want beach-town dining and a free island trolley. Walkability depends on your rental address. Compare rentals for your dates and group.

Longboat Key suits travelers who prefer a quieter residential or resort setting. It has on-island restaurants, groceries and public transportation options. Check the location and complete total of the rental you are considering.

They're connected by the Longboat Pass Bridge, so you can explore both. Allow for bridge openings and traffic rather than a fixed crossing time.

People ask us this one a lot. And honestly, the answer depends on what kind of trip you're planning — because these two islands couldn't feel more different despite being literally next to each other.

Anna Maria Island is the island where you might walk to dinner in flip-flops, grab ice cream on Pine Avenue, and spend the afternoon on the beach. Longboat Key offers a more residential feel, with private homes, condos and resorts spread along the coast. Both can work for a family or a couple; the exact address and the outings you want matter more than the island label.

01The Beach Situation (It's Not Even Close to the Same Experience)

Both islands have Gulf beaches, but access, facilities and the day's water conditions can change your experience. Choose the beach and access point before deciding what to bring.

Anna Maria Island beaches include Manatee Public Beach and Coquina Beach, with lifeguards, restrooms and parking. Coquina also has grills and picnic facilities. Bean Point offers a different north-end setting, but it is not one of the county’s lifeguarded beaches. Check the walking route from your rental rather than assuming every beach is nearby.

Longboat Key beaches have designated public access points with different parking arrangements. Check the town’s access map and the facilities at your chosen entrance. The town states that its beaches are not monitored by lifeguards; a quiet-looking stretch of sand is not a guarantee of safe swimming or privacy.

{{ uiIcon("info") }}Local tip: Greer Island, also called Beer Can Island, is at the north end of Longboat Key. For land access, approach from the Longboat Key side and check North Shore Road access and posted conditions. For a lifeguarded beach day, consider Manatee Public Beach or Coquina Beach.

03Restaurants on Both Islands

There are places to eat on both islands. The practical question is which restaurants are close to your rental and whether their current menus and hours suit your group.

On AMI, the Sandbar is at 100 Spring Avenue in the city of Anna Maria. The Waterfront Restaurant in the city of Anna Maria is at 111 South Bay Boulevard, not in Holmes Beach. Check the restaurant’s own menu, opening hours and seating policy before you go.

Longboat Key has on-island dining, including Dry Dock Waterfront Grill and Shore. Shore’s Longboat Key location is at 800 Broadway Street. Compare the route from your rental and the current menu; St. Armands Circle is another option beyond the island’s south end.

{{ uiIcon("info") }}Local tip: A Bradenton stay can include meals on AMI and outings toward St. Armands Circle, with driving time depending on your address and traffic. Consider The Oasis and River House when planning visits to both areas.

04Getting Around: Trolley, OnDemand and Driving

Anna Maria Island has a free trolley that operates 365 days a year. MCAT Route 5 links the island’s north end with Coquina Beach. A rental near your planned stops can make a car-light trip easier; check the actual walk to the trolley, beach and restaurants.

A car can be convenient on Longboat Key, but it is not the only option. The town lists Breeze OnDemand service for Lido/Longboat Key. Check the current service area, hours, fare and booking arrangements before relying on it, and check the walking route from your rental.

05The Vibe Check (This Is What Really Decides It)

Anna Maria Island feels like...

A small beach town that happens to be on an island. Flip-flops and sunscreen. Town-center outings, beach time and a trolley ride can be part of the same day. Pick the rental address around the places you want to reach on foot.

Longboat Key feels like...

A quieter residential coast, with condos, private homes and resorts. It can suit a slower trip, but the experience depends on your building, beach access and the places you want to visit. Check those details rather than expecting the whole island to feel private or empty.
