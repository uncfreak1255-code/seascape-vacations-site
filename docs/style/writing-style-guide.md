# Seascape writing: start here

## Sources and scope

Read [voice](voice.md), [banned patterns](banned-patterns.md), and
[approved examples](approved-examples.md) before drafting. These are the maintained
editorial rules; this entry point replaces the March 2026 duplicate phrase lists,
hook formulas, fictional local persona, and sentence, heading, proof and CTA quotas.
The [content quality gate](../process/content-quality-gate.md) still governs
briefs, factual checks, answer structure and independent Voice Editor approval.
Existing route-specific requirements remain in force.

OpenSEO writing preferences are a concise projection of these Site-owned sources.
They do not replace the brief, source verification or publication workflow.
For a standalone design export, the three sibling style files travel with this
entry point; repository process links name the owning workflow, not copied authority.

## Write for the decision

- Answer the reader's question in the opening paragraph. Keep the answer useful
  when quoted alone. Preserve required answer blocks and comparison tables.
- Guest guides: distinguish the situations that change the recommendation, then
  give concrete options and why each fits. Retain useful local details.
- Destination comparisons: say who should choose each place and the tradeoff;
  give a relevant next step, such as comparing homes on the same dates.
- Owner pages: lead with the owner's economic or operational benefit, then show
  how the service supports it. Use plain owner language and the approved offer.
- Put review dates, methodology and dataset limits in labeled proof copy below
  the hook. Keep any qualification that changes the decision next to its claim.
- Use descriptive links and a relevant CTA from the active brief. Their number
  and placement follow the page's purpose and existing requirements, not a
  universal quota. Headings should help readers find answers.

## Sound natural without manufacturing experience

Use direct sentences, concrete nouns, contractions where natural, and paragraphs
with one clear point. Let rhythm and length follow meaning. Remove filler rather
than swapping it for a stock conversational bridge. Do not force fragments,
rotate hook formulas, vary headings for a quota, or promise to beat AI detectors.
Keep Seascape's practical local voice without inventing a biography or visits.

Specificity must earn its place. Verify property facts against
`src/_data/properties.js` and its fallback `src/_data/properties-fallback.json`;
use the owning approved source for business claims. Dates, fees, travel times,
prices, availability, reviews and experience claims need appropriate evidence.
Do not copy example numbers or infer current facts from an old article. Scope
comparisons and superlatives to their evidence; omit unsupported savings or
universal “best” claims. An opinion is not proof of firsthand experience.

## Review and learn

Use the existing content-quality rubric's eight dimensions as an advisory review:
information gain versus the SERP, answer-first extractability, scannable AEO
structure, named-source statistical density, E-E-A-T signals, freshness, entity
and destination clarity, and voice with proof boundaries intact. Record strong, weak or
missing with an excerpt and a repair; mark an unavailable dimension unassessable.
Do not combine the dimensions into a numeric SEO score or add a new release gate.

The [2026-10-22 blind comparison](../research/writing-comparison-2026-10-22/README.md)
favored bounded revisions on all three sampled page types. It supports trying
answer-first, plain, benefit-led copy. It does not prove ranking or conversion
lift, validate the underlying facts, or approve the excerpts for publication.
Future page changes still need their own brief and owning verification.

OpenSEO reports use the same clarity and claim discipline, with their report
structure, evidence dates, limitations and decisions retained. Public-page hooks
and CTAs are not a required report format.
