# Approved Examples

These are reference patterns already present in the repo. Copy the thinking, not the exact sentences.

## `src/guides/booking-direct-vacation-rentals.html`

- Approved pattern: fast economic framing.
- Why it works: it opens on the real decision point, the fee stack, instead of wandering through generic vacation copy.
- Reuse when: a page wins by showing how the math changes, not by romanticizing the market.

## `src/guides/bradenton-vs-sarasota.html`

- Approved pattern: direct answer plus evidence card plus comparison table.
- Why it works: it tells the reader which base fits which trip, then backs that with specific sources and a structured comparison.
- Reuse when: a page exists to help a reader choose between two legitimate options.

## `src/property-management/index.njk`

- Approved pattern: switcher-first owner framing.
- Why it works: the page asks an owner to request a revenue review instead of drowning them in a generic service list.
- Reuse when: the page is meant to convert owners who already suspect the current setup is leaking money.

## Owner Review Copy

- Approved pattern: explain the decision in plain owner language, not review-template labels.
- Why it works: it tells the owner what they will learn without sounding like an internal worksheet.
- Reuse when: the page, CTA, or helper copy describes what a revenue review comes back with.

Before:

> We mark proven cost, likely cost, and missing information separately before you switch.

After:

> We show where the payout gets thin, which costs deserve a second look, and what to ask before you renew.

## `src/_data/ownerProofAssets.json`

- Approved pattern: one shared proof asset reused across multiple owner pages.
- Why it works: the same owner benchmark can be cited consistently instead of every page inventing its own trust props.
- Reuse when: a stat needs to travel across related owner pages.

## `src/_data/seoPages.json` owner section

- Approved pattern: owner-economics modules.
- Why it works: the pages are structured around leaks, objections, channel cost, and net outcome instead of generic "hospitality excellence" copy.
- Reuse when: the page's job is to make an owner reconsider the current manager.

## Guest Research Page Intros

- Approved pattern: reader decision first, source language second.
- Why it works: the page earns attention by answering what the traveler is trying to decide before it explains how the numbers were built.
- Reuse when: a research page, calculator, or chart asset needs to preserve proof without sounding like an internal methodology note.

Before:

> Interactive planning estimate for Bradenton, Sarasota, and Anna Maria Island trips using the accepted cost-index formulas and proof boundaries.

After:

> Compare Bradenton, Sarasota, and Anna Maria Island trip costs before you book, then use the source notes below to see what the estimate can and cannot claim.

## Guest Comparison CTAs

- Approved pattern: name the next decision, not the generic action.
- Why it works: guest SEO pages should move readers from research into the stay collection that matches the trip shape.
- Reuse when: the page is a guest-facing guide or research asset that should feed stay pages.

Before:

> Contact us to learn more.

After:

> See Bradenton homes near AMI.

## Guest Planning Language

- Approved pattern: translate internal funnel logic into the vacation a guest can picture.
- Why it works: travelers think about quiet mornings, children, restaurants, pools, parking, and time near the beach. They do not think in `trip shapes`, `stay bases`, or `booking paths`.
- Reuse when: a guide moves from destination advice into a relevant Seascape home or collection.

Before:

> Pick the trip shape, then open the right stay base.

After:

> Choose Anna Maria Island for quieter family days. Choose Siesta Key when famous sand, more restaurants, and livelier nights matter more.

Before:

> Named Sarasota-side option

After:

> Want Siesta beach days with your own pool?

## Source Notes

- Approved pattern: proof belongs in a labeled source note below the hook.
- Why it works: it protects claim quality without making the page intro sound like a legal memo.
- Reuse when: a visible page needs to explain dataset size, dates, methodology, source limits, or quote limitations.

Before:

> Proof boundary: This calculator is a planning tool, not a quote.

After:

> Source note: Use this as a planning estimate, not a quote.
