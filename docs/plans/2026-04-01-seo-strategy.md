# Seascape SEO Strategy

Date: 2026-04-01
Scope: Guest direct-booking SEO, owner acquisition SEO, GEO/AEO readiness, and site-structure discipline for `seascape-vacations.com`

## Strategic Call

Do not split the brand onto a second owner domain.

The domain already has authority signals and rankings on one site. The problem is not "one domain serves two audiences." The problem is that the domain still makes Google and AI systems work too hard to understand which pages serve guests, which pages serve owners, and which guides should transfer authority into money pages.

For the next 90 days, the right strategy is:

1. finish canonical convergence
2. turn comparison-guide authority into money-page authority
3. improve owner-page click yield with proof, not generic copy
4. rebuild the two most important stay pages into real commercial landers
5. tighten entity clarity and reviewer treatment only after the on-site targets are stronger

## Business Model Reality

Seascape is a hybrid, not a pure local-service site:

- guest direct-booking platform
- vacation-rental owner acquisition business
- local comparison/guide publisher

That means the strategy cannot be publisher-only, local-service-only, or inventory-only.

## What Is Working

- The comparison-guide cluster is the domain's strongest nonbrand wedge.
- Technical crawlability is mostly intact: sitemap, redirects, and JSON-LD validation already exist.
- AI crawlers are allowed and `llms.txt` is already deployed.
- Licensing and fee terms are real owner-intent opportunities, validated by DataForSEO.
- Anna Maria Island stay demand is large enough to justify a serious commercial page, not a thin template.

## What Is Not Working

- Legacy URL variants still split authority.
- Guides earn attention, but money pages do not cash it in.
- Owner pages rank without enough proof to earn the click.
- Stay pages exist without enough commercial depth to compete.
- Entity clarity is still soft: thin `sameAs`, inconsistent visible reviewer treatment, and too few Person-backed pages.

## Strategic Thesis

One domain. Two funnels. One guide wedge.

That means:

- the root domain and homepage stay guest-first
- `/property-management/` behaves like a clearly separated owner funnel on the same domain
- `/guides/` exists to win comparison and local research demand, then route it into the right commercial page
- no new page gets built unless it strengthens a money page or resolves an authority leak

## Audience Priorities

### Guest side

Objective:
- capture comparison and destination demand
- route to direct-booking stay pages

Primary page types:
- comparison guides
- destination guides
- stay category pages
- property pages

### Owner side

Objective:
- capture management, fee, licensing, and platform-intent searches
- convert into owner inquiries

Primary page types:
- owner hub
- Florida owner-intent pages
- local owner-market pages
- proof assets and benchmark pages

## Keyword Strategy

### Guest priorities

Validated by DataForSEO:

1. `anna maria island vacation rentals`
2. `anna maria island beach vacation rentals`
3. `holmes beach vacation rentals`
4. `bradenton beach vacation rentals`

Implication:
- do not scatter effort across many stay variants
- build a smaller number of stronger category landers

### Owner priorities

Validated by DataForSEO:

1. `vacation rental license florida`
2. `florida vacation rental license`
3. `rental property management fees`
4. `short term rental management fees`
5. `vacation rental management florida`

Implication:
- licensing and fees are real clusters
- broad Florida management-company expansion is not
- VRBO stays a support/conversion page unless real query breadth appears in GSC

## GEO / AEO Strategy

Do not use the homepage as the AI-search scoreboard.

The best AI-shaped surfaces today are:

- `/guides/bradenton-vs-sarasota/`
- `/guides/anna-maria-island-vs-siesta-key/`

The GEO strategy is:

1. keep using comparison guides as the acquisition wedge
2. force those pages to transfer authority to money pages
3. add one owner proof asset and one guest proof asset worth citing
4. improve visible person/reviewer treatment on top pages
5. expand off-site entity signals only after the on-site pages are stronger

## What To Stop Doing

- Stop treating new page volume as the default answer.
- Stop using the homepage as the main AI-visibility benchmark.
- Stop broad metadata cleanup on pages with no impressions.
- Stop building owner page variants unless GSC or DataForSEO proves the term cluster.
- Stop doing off-site entity work before the owner and stay pages are stronger.

## Decision Rule

Before any new SEO build, ask:

1. Is there already a version of this topic getting impressions?
2. Is the current failure identity, click yield, or topical depth?
3. Does the page strengthen a money page or just create another URL?
4. Can the current test layer catch the most likely regression?

If question 3 is "mostly another URL," do not build it yet.
