## Instantly AI Setup Notes

**Sending schedule:** Weekday mornings 7-9am recipient's timezone
**Daily send limit:** Start at 30/day per account, ramp to 50 after warmup
**Unsubscribe link:** Required — Instantly adds automatically
**Tracking:** Open tracking ON, link tracking OFF (reduces spam flags)
**Warmup:** Run 14 days minimum before launching sequences
**Lead source:** Build lists from Muck Rack, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, or manual byline research
**Exclusions:** Remove anyone who's already covered Seascape or has "no pitches" in their bio
