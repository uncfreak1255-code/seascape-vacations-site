## Result and adoption

The evaluator preferred the revision in each case. It favored a direct decision
and concrete alternatives on the rainy-day guide; a faster who-should-choose-what
answer on the comparison; and owner benefits before the service inventory.
Both comparison arms and both owner arms were strong on all assessable dimensions.
The rainy-day baseline drew concerns about unsupported superlatives within the
supplied packet. Those concerns are not independent findings that its claims are
false. Author or freshness evidence absent from an excerpt was unassessable.

Adopt answer-first choices, useful local detail, plain audience language,
benefit-led owner framing and relevant next steps. Remove the stale guide's
stock transitions, fictional persona, AI-detector claims and universal quotas.
Retain existing briefs, claim checks, extractable answer structure and independent
Voice Editor approval. Local OpenSEO receives a concise projection of the
Site-owned guidance; report evidence and limitations remain explicit.

## Limits and verification

This trial does not establish rankings, conversion lift, reader preference across
audiences, full-page quality, freshness of attractions, or approval to publish
the sample copy. It does not compare against external top-ranking articles.
The source-fact sheets are frozen inputs, not a new factual audit.

The existing Site release and merge checks verify the source change. A focused
design-export dependency check verifies the standalone style files travel with
the guide. A separate source reviewer checks the reconciled guidance and a fresh
instruction read; local OpenSEO must be read back after its preference patch.
No schedules or public pages are changed by this work.
