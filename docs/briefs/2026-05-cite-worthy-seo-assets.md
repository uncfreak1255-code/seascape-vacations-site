# Cite-Worthy SEO Asset Roadmap - May 2026

This brief translates the linkable-content idea into Seascape-specific assets. These are planned opportunities, not shipped claims.

## Current Shipped Base

The first branch should harden the two existing research assets:

- `/research/gulf-coast-vacation-booking-trends-2026/`
- `/research/real-cost-florida-beach-vacation-bradenton-sarasota-ami-2026/`

Both should be treated as citation pages: methodology, source date, canonical URL, quotable findings, share snippet, and internal links to stay or owner money pages.

## Asset 1: Gulf Coast Vacation Rental Booking Benchmark Report

Audience: owners, STR operators, local real estate writers, travel writers.

Core proof required:

- confirmed booking count
- reservation count versus confirmed booking count
- source system and date range
- property count and market coverage
- ADR by season
- channel mix
- booking lead time
- waterfront premium

Needed before expansion:

- one approved proof block for the 545-booking dataset
- consistent use of "1,492 reservations" versus "545 confirmed bookings"
- chart images or simple embeddable tables
- media/contact instructions

## Asset 2: Florida Gulf Coast Vacation Cost Index

Audience: guests, family travel bloggers, local tourism writers, comparison/query pages.

Core proof required:

- family size and trip length assumption
- lodging source
- restaurant/menu check date
- grocery price check date
- activity/vendor price check date
- transportation price check date

Needed before expansion:

- clear "not guaranteed quote" proof boundary
- chart for budget/mid-range/comfortable trip
- short social snippets
- internal links to Bradenton, AMI, and Sarasota stay money pages

## Asset 3: Owner Fee + Revenue Leak Benchmark

Audience: rental owners, investor blogs, local real estate agents.

Working title: `2026 Florida Gulf Coast Vacation Rental Management Fee Benchmark`

Core proof required:

- management-fee range with source
- OTA guest service fees
- OTA host fees
- direct-booking leakage model
- cleaning and maintenance cost ranges
- sample owner net calculation

Do not ship until:

- owner claims are sourced from approved owner proof assets
- the model separates guest-paid fees from owner-paid fees
- every percentage has a source or is labeled as a scenario

Money destination:

- `/property-management/`
- `/property-management/vacation-rental-management-fees-florida/`
- `/property-management/maximize-vacation-rental-income-florida/`

## Asset 4: Interactive / Visual Asset

Working options:

- Bradenton vs Sarasota vs AMI vacation cost calculator
- Gulf Coast rental fee calculator
- direct-booking savings calculator

Minimum viable version:

- static form with client-side math
- prefilled assumptions
- no lead capture gate
- shareable result copy
- clear source/date note

Do not build before:

- final formulas are approved
- assumptions are documented
- calculator output avoids guaranteed savings language

## Asset 5: Regulation / Compliance Tracker

Audience: owners, real estate agents, STR compliance writers.

Coverage:

- Anna Maria
- Holmes Beach
- Bradenton Beach
- Bradenton / Manatee County
- Sarasota / Sarasota County
- Longboat Key

Core proof required:

- official municipal source URL
- last checked date
- permit/license requirement
- occupancy/noise/parking constraints
- minimum-stay notes where relevant

Do not ship before:

- every row links to official sources
- page has an explicit update cadence
- legal disclaimer says it is an owner planning guide, not legal advice

## Distribution Notes

Do not stop at publishing. Each asset needs:

- citation block
- share snippet
- visual table/chart
- contact email
- internal links to money pages
- outreach list for relevant local writers, owner groups, real estate agents, and tourism blogs

The strongest wedge is not "ultimate guide" volume. It is local data plus owner economics plus guest cost tradeoffs.
