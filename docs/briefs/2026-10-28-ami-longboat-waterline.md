| The Waterfront is in Holmes Beach | False. Restaurant gives111South Bay Blvd, Anna Maria: https://thewaterfrontrestaurant.net/contact-us/ |
| One island has restaurants/Longboat has only12 | Unsupported count and false categorical headline. Both have dining.
