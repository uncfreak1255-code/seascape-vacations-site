## What To Stop Doing

- Do not treat free directories as the main growth engine.
- Do not send generic “please link to me” emails.
- Do not pitch the homepage when a guide or owner page is the stronger destination.
- Do not spend time on targets that cannot plausibly send either authority or qualified traffic.

- Bradenton vs Sarasota pricing and family travel tradeoffs

- the market-analysis page gives you something quotable instead of pure opinion

### 3. Creator / Blogger Resource Inclusion

Do not pitch random bloggers. Pitch creators who already publish:

- Florida beach comparisons
- Gulf Coast family travel itineraries
- “where to stay near Anna Maria Island” roundups
- Sarasota vs Bradenton relocation or travel content

Best target pages:

- `/guides/bradenton-vs-sarasota/`
- `/guides/anna-maria-island-vs-siesta-key/`

What makes this pitch work now:

- both pages have direct-answer intros
- both pages have strong internal next steps
- both pages are specific enough to be cited as planning resources

### 4. Data-Backed Guest Posts

Guest posting only works if you bring a sharper angle than generic travel fluff.

Use these:

- “Bradenton vs Sarasota: what actually changes for families and budgets”
- “Why direct booking matters more on the Gulf Coast than travelers realize”
- “What vacation-rental owners get wrong about the Bradenton-Sarasota corridor”

Best destinations:

- Florida travel blogs
- relocation blogs
- short-term-rental industry blogs
- local market commentary blogs

### 5. Relationship-Based Link Recovery

The easiest free links are often from people already in your operating orbit.

Priority relationship buckets:

- attractions already mentioned in guides
- restaurants or activity providers already recommended in guides
- vendors or partners who already receive guest referrals
- guests or owners with blogs, newsletters, or company resource pages

This is not about begging for links. It is about converting existing referral relationships into explicit citations.

## Priority Order

1. Journalist query responses
2. Resource-link reversal from cited attractions and institutions
3. Creator/resource inclusion for the two comparison guides
4. Data-backed guest posts
5. Relationship-based link recovery

## 14-Day Sprint

### Day 1-2

- set up one daily journalist-query routine
- prepare 3 quote blocks from the market analysis and comparison guides
- finalize the first 8 free targets in [free-backlink-priority-targets-2026-03.csv](/Users/sawbeck/Projects/seascape-vacations-site/docs/strategy/free-backlink-priority-targets-2026-03.csv#L1)

### Day 3-5

- send 4 resource-link reversal emails
- send 4 creator/resource inclusion pitches
- log every touch in [backlink-outreach-tracker-template.csv](/Users/sawbeck/Projects/seascape-vacations-site/docs/strategy/backlink-outreach-tracker-template.csv#L1)

### Day 6-10

- send 2 guest-post pitches
- answer at least 3 journalist queries
- follow up on all non-responders once

### Day 11-14

- measure: replies, links earned, placements promised
- double down on the channel with the best response rate
- kill any channel that produces zero replies after 8-10 quality touches

## Kill Switch

If you send:

- 10 quality creator/resource pitches with zero replies
- 5 attraction/resource asks with zero replies
- 5 journalist responses with zero placements

then the message is not the only problem. The asset choice is wrong, and you should switch to a new pitch angle or new target set instead of grinding harder.
