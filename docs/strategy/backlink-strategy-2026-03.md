## Why This Matters

The site ranks #2.5 for "bradenton vs sarasota" (23,671 impressions) with ZERO backlinks
to that page. Content quality alone got us here. Even 10-15 quality backlinks could push
dozens of page-2 keywords onto page 1, potentially doubling organic traffic from ~860 to 1,500+ monthly visits.

## TIER 2: Content-Driven Link Building (Week 2-4) — Expected: 5-10 links

### 4. Pitch Top Guides as Resources
Your comparison guides are genuinely useful. Pitch them to sites that link to similar content.

**bradenton-vs-sarasota.html** (position 2.5, 23K impressions):
- Pitch to travel bloggers writing "Florida Gulf Coast" content
- Pitch to relocation sites (movoto.com, niche.com, areavibes.com)
- Pitch to real estate blogs covering Bradenton/Sarasota markets

**anna-maria-island-vs-siesta-key.html** (position 3.2, 14K impressions):
- Pitch to beach comparison roundup articles
- Pitch to family travel blogs
- Submit to travel subreddits as a resource (r/florida, r/travel)

### 5. HARO / Quoted.ai / Connectively
Sign up for journalist query services. You have unique local expertise:
- Respond to queries about "Florida vacations", "beach destinations", "Gulf Coast travel"
- Cite your market report data (rental pricing, occupancy trends)
- Each placement typically earns a DA 50-90 backlink

### 6. Guest Posts on Travel/Real Estate Blogs
Pitch original articles to:
- Local Bradenton/Sarasota news blogs
- Vacation rental industry blogs (Hostfully, AirDNA blog, Lodgify)
- Florida travel blogs
- Topic ideas: "Why Bradenton Is the Best-Kept Secret on Florida's Gulf Coast"
  "Anna Maria Island: What Vacasa Won't Tell You About Booking Direct"

## TIER 3: Relationship Building (Month 2-3) — Expected: 5-10 links

### 7. Local Business Cross-Promotion
Every local attraction, restaurant, or activity you mention in your guides is a potential link partner.

**High-priority targets (already cited in your guides):**
- Mote Marine Laboratory (mote.org) — mentioned in AMI vs Siesta Key guide
- The Bishop Museum of Science and Nature — mentioned in guides
- Ringling Museum — mentioned in bradenton-vs-sarasota
- Myakka River State Park — mentioned in guides
- Local restaurants featured in your dining sections

**Ask:** "We feature your business in our visitor guide at [URL]. Would you consider
linking to our guide from your visitor resources or partners page?"

### 8. Property/Guest Review Links
- Ask past guests to mention your site in their travel blog posts
- Create a "Share Your Seascape Story" page that incentivizes user-generated content
- Respond to forum threads about Bradenton/AMI vacations with helpful info + link

### 9. Local News & PR
- Pitch seasonal stories to Bradenton Herald, Sarasota Herald-Tribune
- "Vacation rental market data" angle using your market report page
- Sponsor or participate in local events (beach cleanups, etc.) for event page backlinks
