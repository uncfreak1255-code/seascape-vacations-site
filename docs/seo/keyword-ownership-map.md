## Top 3 Gaps

**1. No broad Bradenton or Sarasota hub page**
The most-searched destination terms ("Bradenton vacation rentals," "Sarasota vacation rentals") have no owning page. All Bradenton and Sarasota pages are faceted long-tail. A `/stays/bradenton-vacation-rentals/` and `/stays/sarasota-vacation-rentals/` hub would consolidate internal link authority and capture head-of-funnel traffic that current pages miss entirely.

**2. Girls trip and bachelorette cluster is fully noindexed**
`/stays/girl-trip-vacation-rentals-florida/` and `/stays/bachelor-bachelorette-rentals-florida/` are built but noindexed. These are high-commercial-intent group trip terms. If the content is thin, fix it and index — don't leave the cluster dark.

**3. No Florida-wide pet-friendly page**
Pet-friendly is only covered for Bradenton. The Oasis is pet-friendly — a Florida-wide or AMI-area pet-friendly page would capture a large, high-intent segment (pet owners have few options and search specifically). This is a direct revenue gap.

## Top 3 Cannibalization Risks

**1. Homepage vs. `/stays/florida-gulf-coast-vacation-rentals/` on broad Gulf Coast terms**
Homepage title is "Luxury Vacation Rentals AMI, Bradenton & Sarasota." The stays page targets "Florida Gulf Coast Vacation Rentals." Both pages are indexed and both send signals for the same family of head terms. Neither clearly owns the other. Fix: homepage claims the brand + book-direct angle; the stays page claims the destination discovery angle with a clear canonical role.

**2. Romantic getaway vs. honeymoon on AMI**
`/stays/romantic-getaway-anna-maria-island/` and `/stays/honeymoon-rentals-anna-maria-island/` target nearly identical users (couples, AMI, private stay). Google will pick one or neither. Fix: one page should redirect to the other, or differentiate with intent (honeymoon = first trip together, romantic = returning couples). Currently both are active with no clear distinction.

**3. 4BR + sleeps-12 pages competing across the same three properties**
`/stays/4-bedroom-vacation-rentals-florida/` and `/stays/vacation-rentals-sleeps-12-florida/` both exist, and three of the five properties are 4BR/12-guest homes (Dockside Dreams, Sarasota Luxe, River House). Both pages likely surface the same properties and rank for overlapping queries ("4 bedroom rental Florida" vs "sleeps 12 rental Florida" have nearly identical SERP intent). These pages need differentiated content angles or one should funnel into the other.
