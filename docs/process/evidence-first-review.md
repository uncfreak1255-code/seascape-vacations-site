# Evidence-First Review

Default stance: `not ready` until evidence says otherwise.

## Public Copy Review

- prove the first paragraph answers the real decision
- prove the page uses approved proof instead of vague authority theater
- prove the page keeps the required internal links and CTA target
- prove banned language and internal workflow wording are absent

## Launch Review

- prove the route builds
- prove lint, tests, and route smoke passed
- prove the live or local rendered page matches the intended copy
- prove the conversion step still works or remains correctly gated

## Closeout Receipt

- what changed
- what evidence passed
- what remains unverified
- whether the page is ready, blocked, or still needs work
