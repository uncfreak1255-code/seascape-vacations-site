## Approved positioning

Text for OpenSEO's `positioning` section; citations retain its provenance.
Current Hub `origin/main` at `a1634f19d09f6d1486d6ea4314b98f004053eb84`
was checked on October 22 against the pinned excerpts below. The intervening
property changes concern pool heat and do not change the positioning claims.
The original evidence dates remain unchanged.

Seascape Vacations is a boutique, locally owned vacation-rental business with
seven live homes in Bradenton and Sarasota, Florida. The website serves
guests choosing a vacation home and homeowners considering property
management. Its business goals include direct bookings and owner acquisition.
[context/seascape-properties.md:4-9](https://github.com/uncfreak1255-code/seascape-hub/blob/3d562c67bc1a45708c6c19c5f3215769abd9cad1/context/seascape-properties.md#L4-L9),
[context/seascape-properties.md:259-260](https://github.com/uncfreak1255-code/seascape-hub/blob/3d562c67bc1a45708c6c19c5f3215769abd9cad1/context/seascape-properties.md#L259-L260),
[context/seascape-properties.md:418-419](https://github.com/uncfreak1255-code/seascape-hub/blob/3d562c67bc1a45708c6c19c5f3215769abd9cad1/context/seascape-properties.md#L418-L419),
[context/market-position.md:70-71](https://github.com/uncfreak1255-code/seascape-hub/blob/3d562c67bc1a45708c6c19c5f3215769abd9cad1/context/market-position.md#L70-L71)

For homeowners, emphasize attentive local management, guest care, and proactive
communication about revenue, reviews, cleaning, and maintenance. Management
pricing follows the home's evaluation and agreement; there is no universal
15% offer. Present bookings, owner economics, and care as the priorities the
service addresses, without promising higher returns or treating AI as the
reason to choose Seascape.
[context/market-position.md:30-52](https://github.com/uncfreak1255-code/seascape-hub/blob/3d562c67bc1a45708c6c19c5f3215769abd9cad1/context/market-position.md#L30-L52)

For guests, help match the group to the particular home's location, capacity,
and amenities. Dockside Dreams is the verified waterfront home; River House
is near Warner Bayou and has no private dock. Blue House's generic geography
tags do not establish waterfront access. Keep channel differences explicit:
Sarasota Luxe's direct-booking cap is twelve guests, while Airbnb and Vrbo
have an eight-guest cap. These examples illustrate why portfolio-wide
amenity and capacity promises are unsuitable.
[context/seascape-properties.md:22-32](https://github.com/uncfreak1255-code/seascape-hub/blob/3d562c67bc1a45708c6c19c5f3215769abd9cad1/context/seascape-properties.md#L22-L32),
[context/seascape-properties.md:415-419](https://github.com/uncfreak1255-code/seascape-hub/blob/3d562c67bc1a45708c6c19c5f3215769abd9cad1/context/seascape-properties.md#L415-L419)
