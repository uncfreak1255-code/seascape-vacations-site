# Shared Waterline stay collections

The shared `src/stays/stays.njk` template uses `guest-site stay-collection` and
`src/css/stay-collection.css`. Stay bodies have no legacy typography exception:
use Instrument Serif, paper/ink/soft surfaces, open ruled sections and 4px
controls from Waterline. Keep every existing collection word, SEO field, photo,
section anchor, booking destination and tracking attribute. The shared header
and footer remain the existing guest partials.

The collection opening pairs its original title/description/actions with its
existing real photograph; property lists use open image/spec/action treatments,
and FAQ answers remain readable without JavaScript. On phones, retain all
comparison values, 44px controls and content clearance above the sticky action.
The stay template and styles are in the design scan; the family stay route is
a representative visual/floor route, and all generated stay leaves retain the
whole-page design-floor body class. No inline legacy style shell or new
grandfathered stay exemption may substitute for this shared treatment.

The family-vacation-rentals-anna-maria-island leaf uses Sawyer's approved
guide-first browse layout: the existing short answer precedes the native
catalog trip form, followed by its four matched homes, full article and FAQs.
`stay-family.css` scopes that layout to `stay-flow flow-browse`. The native
catalog form/comparison partials and catalog.js retain dates, guests, filters,
honest availability states and distinct booking/details actions. This family
exception changes ordering and booking actions, preserving original facts,
photos, SEO and internal destinations. Other leaves retain the shared frame.
