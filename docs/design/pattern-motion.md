# Pattern motion (November 2026)

`DESIGN.md` gives motion three jobs and links here for the one route past them.
This file carries the same authority. Sawyer approved the wider rule on
November 22, 2026 (site issue #1041, item 2).

## The route

A pattern in the [component catalog](component-patterns.md) may use motion
beyond the three jobs on a production page when both hold:

1. Sawyer selected the pattern from a working preview, and its catalog entry
   says so (status `selected`, or `implemented` once that choice is built). An
   agent choosing a pattern for a page is not a selection.
2. The entry records the motion he saw: what moves, what starts it, its
   duration and curve, and what reduced-motion and no-JavaScript readers get
   instead.

Build the motion the entry records, on that pattern only. The entry holds the
timing; this file keeps no second copy. A new or changed motion goes back
through a labeled exploration and a new selection.

The page-turn booklet is the worked case: selected November 17, 2026, with its
turn recorded under "Selected page-turn booklet" in the catalog.

## What stays as it was

- Every limit in `DESIGN.md` binds pattern motion too: browser-native
  technology only, the banned effects, complete reduced-motion and
  no-JavaScript paths, and no animation that delays booking or is needed to
  reveal essential information.
- A `reference` or `explored` pattern, a pattern with no recorded selection,
  and any motion no entry records stay in a labeled exploration.
- The scale rules in [scales, states and forms](scales-states-forms.md) are
  unchanged. A pattern's timing is never a new default for controls.

## Entrances

Under this route an entry may record an entrance that plays once when the
pattern first appears, with the content readable before it starts. Motion that
loops, or that advances content on its own, stays banned as autoplay.
