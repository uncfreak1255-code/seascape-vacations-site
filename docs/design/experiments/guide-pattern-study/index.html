Reusable guide vocabulary

Four patterns worth keeping on the shelf

These are shape studies, not a component API. Each still needs a page-specific dataset, accessible table and rendered review.

GuideLine

Rounded spline line

Published observations over time. Keep visible points and honest intervals; no smoothing that invents a trend.

GuideBars

Rounded pill pillars

Comparable amounts with a shared zero baseline and direct end labels.

GuideRange

Range band

Published lower and upper values. Name both endpoints; do not imply a forecast or precise average.

GuideStacked · hold

Stacked tones

Use only when the published parts form a meaningful whole. The weather fixture does not justify it.
