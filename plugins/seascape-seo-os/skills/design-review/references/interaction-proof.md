# Interaction Proof

Capture the state transition needed to substantiate the claim:

- **Navigation or CTA:** before/after state and final URL or scroll target.
- **Form:** empty, filled, validation, and success states using authorized safe
  fixtures. A visual audit does not authorize real lead or guest submissions.
- **Accordion, menu, modal, carousel, filter:** closed/collapsed and open/expanded
  states, plus the relevant selection or navigation result.
- **Mobile menu or sticky CTA:** closed/open states and tap-target check.
- **Keyboard:** focused state and visible result after the expected key action;
  use `accessibility` for component-specific keyboard and screen-reader proof.

For sticky/fixed UI and long scrolling pages, include viewport captures where
full-page screenshots hide the actual behavior.
