## Problems & Pain Points

### Guest

**Core problem:** Booking a group vacation rental on Airbnb/VRBO is expensive (service fees stack up fast for large groups), and it's hard to trust what you're actually getting. The photos look good but the host is remote and the reviews could be gamed.

**Why current solutions fall short:**
- Airbnb/VRBO: 15-20% service fee on top of the nightly rate. For a 7-night stay at $450/night, that's $500+ in fees before the trip starts.
- On-island AMI properties: Tighter inventory, higher nightly rates, parking friction, walkout beach access doesn't justify the premium for most groups.
- Generic search results: Hard to know which operator is actually local and accountable.

**What it costs them:** $400-$700 in avoidable fees per trip. Uncertainty and logistics overhead.

**Emotional tension:** "I'm spending $3,000+ on this vacation and I still don't know if it'll actually be what the photos show."

### Owner

**Core problem:** High occupancy that doesn't translate to the net revenue they expected. The leak is usually OTA fee drag, weak dynamic pricing during premium weeks, and turnover quality issues that drive review scores down.

**Emotional tension:** "My calendar is full but I feel like I'm working for Airbnb, not for myself."

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Airbnb and VRBO listings of similar Bradenton/Sarasota homes — fall short because they add 15-20% guest service fees, host accountability is variable, and there's no local operator behind the listing.

**Direct (local):** Other local property managers (e.g., Gulf Coast Vacation Rentals, Vacasa local operations) — fall short because they manage too many properties to give individual attention, pricing is often weak, and they don't specialize in the direct-book angle.

**Indirect:** Staying on Anna Maria Island itself — falls short for the value-conscious group because inventory is tighter and more expensive; parking is difficult for big groups.

**Indirect:** Hotel/resort booking — falls short for groups of 10+ because price-per-room adds up fast and there's no private pool or communal cooking setup.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- Direct-book pricing: 10-15% cheaper than the same homes listed on Airbnb/VRBO
- Small portfolio: 5 homes means actual attention per property, not a managed-at-scale operation

**Why customers choose us over Airbnb:**

**Why customers choose us over other local managers:**

## Switching Dynamics

**Push (what drives guests away from Airbnb/VRBO):**
- Sticker shock when fees appear at checkout
- Past experience with a remote host who was unresponsive
- Difficulty verifying if the photos match reality

**Pull (what attracts them to Seascape direct):**
- Visible fee savings (concrete math — $450/night vs. $530+ with OTA fees)
- Local host contact before and during stay
- Transparent tradeoff guidance — no over-promising

**Habit (what keeps them booking OTAs):**
- Habit: "I always use Airbnb, it's easier"
- Trust: "At least Airbnb has a refund policy"
- Familiarity: Search is easier on OTA platforms

**Anxiety (what worries them about booking direct):**
- "What if something goes wrong — who do I call?"
- "Is this a real company or a one-person operation?"
- "Am I protected if the home is misrepresented?"
